## What it is

My personal toolbox for contests: algorithms and data structures written from scratch,
then tested in real contest conditions on Codeforces.

Writing each structure myself, instead of copying a reference implementation, is how I learned the details
that matter: invariants, edge cases, and the constant factors that decide whether a solution passes.
