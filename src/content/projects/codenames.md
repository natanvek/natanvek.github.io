## Why

July 2020, lockdown, and a group of friends who wanted to keep playing board games together.
So I built a server-hosted, multiplayer version of **Codenames** we could play over Zoom.

## Why it matters to me

This was my **first project involving servers**: shared game state, multiple clients, and real users
(my friends) finding every bug.
