## Overview

Alongside my studies, I've worked with a range of clients on backend and tooling projects.

### Numetri: AI-powered e-commerce analytics
Built backend integrations and data pipelines connecting e-commerce ERPs (**SellerCloud, Ordoro, Goflow**)
to an **MCP-based AI analytics layer**, so sellers can ask questions about sales, inventory, and profitability
in plain language.

### SCloudConsulting: e-commerce reporting
Developed custom reporting and analytics for large e-commerce sellers, turning operational data into
client-facing dashboards and business reports.

### Guzman Accesorios: retail operations
Internal tools for order and inventory tracking across branches for a long-running auto and 4x4 accessories
retailer, plus catalog and stock data support for its nationwide online sales.

### Sobol Viajes: travel agency workflows
Internal tooling for quotes, itineraries, and client follow-ups.

### LimudBA: non-profit events
Web tooling for event registration and logistics for a volunteer-run Jewish cultural organization in Buenos Aires.
