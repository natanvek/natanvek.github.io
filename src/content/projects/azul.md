## The game

A virtual version of the board game **Azul**, playable in the browser.

## The AI

The opponent uses **Monte Carlo Tree Search**: it simulates many random playouts from the current position
and focuses its search on the moves that look most promising, balancing exploration and exploitation.

It ranked in the **top 30 on azee.mattle.online**, an online Azul platform.
